I am a Research Scientist at [Apple Machine Learning Research](https://machinelearning.apple.com/), led by [Samy Bengio](https://bengio.abracadoudou.com/). My research spans **generative modeling and large language models**: diffusion and flow-based models and their distillation into fast one-step generators, diffusion language models and efficient LLM decoding, and self-improvement of LLMs for code generation and reasoning. More broadly, I am interested in making generative models efficient, scalable and principled, drawing on probabilistic and Bayesian methods.

Before joining Apple, I received my PhD in Statistics and Data Sciences from the [University of Texas at Austin](https://stat.utexas.edu/), advised by [Mingyuan Zhou](https://mingyuanzhou.github.io/). During my PhD I was a research intern at Microsoft Research, working with [Pengcheng He](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/penhe/), [Weizhu Chen](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/wzchen/) and [Chunyuan Li](https://chunyuan.li/), and at ByteDance AML, working with [Jianbo Yuan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=B1EhbCsAAAAJ), [Quanzeng You](https://qzyou.github.io/), [Guanghan Ning](http://guanghan.info/) and [Hongxia Yang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=iJlC5mMAAAAJ).

I obtained my bachelor's and master's degrees from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/), advised by [Ya Zhang](https://mediabrain.sjtu.edu.cn/yazhang/), through a Sino-French program that also took me to [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/en/). I was also a research intern at Johns Hopkins University with [Lingxi Xie](http://lingxixie.com/) and [Alan Yuille](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~ayuille/).
